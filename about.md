Mem0 extracts and manages memories.

Qdrant stores vector representations and associated memory data locally.

OpenAI generates responses using retrieved memories as context.

Only the user's message is offered to Mem0 for durable-memory extraction, under a policy that prioritizes stable preferences, ongoing goals, recurring instructions, and user-confirmed facts. Assistant answers and temporary conversation details are not sent as durable-memory input. Finance-related memories must retain user-stated provenance and reporting period.
